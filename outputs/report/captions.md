# Figures and tables

Development (out-of-fold) results unless marked TEST. Generated by backend/report_figures.py.

**Table 1.** Characteristics of the included patients, overall and by hospital (PhysioNet/CinC 2019). 426 patients were excluded because sepsis was already labelled at their first hour.  
`tables/Table1_cohort_characteristics.csv`, `.md`

**Table 2.** Performance of the four models on development patients (out-of-fold), at the balanced alert setting (thresholds chosen on development data for about 5 false alarms per 100 non-septic patient-days). Warned in time = an alert in the 12 hours before the sepsis label; AUROC and AUPRC are hour-level.  
`tables/Table2_model_performance.csv`, `.md`

**Table 3.** Septic patients by organ group (partial SOFA, window 24 h before to 6 h after the sepsis label) and the share warned in time by LightGBM at the balanced setting, with Wilson 95% CIs. Attributable (not Unattributable): 51.5% overall; gate (50% in each hospital) passed.  
`tables/Table3_organ_groups.csv`, `.md`

**Table 4.** Performance of the four models on held-out TEST set (final, reported once), at the balanced alert setting (thresholds chosen on development data for about 5 false alarms per 100 non-septic patient-days). Warned in time = an alert in the 12 hours before the sepsis label; AUROC and AUPRC are hour-level.  
`tables/Table4_model_performance_test.csv`, `.md`
